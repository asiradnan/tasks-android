Asir Tasks Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Asir Tasks is a task manager that works entirely offline by default. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

Effective: August 8, 2026 com.asiradnan.asirtasks Developer: Asir Adnan

Overview

Asir Tasks does not collect, share, or sell personal data. All your tasks are stored locally on your device. The internet connection is used only if you choose to log in and enable cloud sync.

Data We Handle

Data Where stored Sent to server?
Task name, status, date & time On-device (Room database) Only when logged in & syncing
JWT access & refresh tokens On-device (DataStore) Never
Username & password Not stored At login only, not retained
Theme preference, last sync time On-device (DataStore) Never

Permissions

The app requests the following permissions to provide its core functionality:

  • INTERNET — used only to sync tasks with the cloud backend when you are logged in. No network requests are made while you are logged out.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — used to accurately schedule local notifications for your tasks so you are reminded exactly on time.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — used to display task reminders to you.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — used to reschedule your task reminders locally if your device is restarted.

No access to camera, microphone, location, contacts, files, or SMS is requested.

Cloud Sync (Optional)

The app works fully offline without an account. If you sign in, your tasks are periodically synced to tasksbackend.asiradnan.com over HTTPS. The sync transmits task name, status, date, time, and a randomly generated UUID. Authentication is handled via accounts.asiradnan.com.

You can log out at any time. Logging out immediately clears all tokens from your device and stops all sync activity.

Authentication & Security

  • Authentication uses standard JWT Bearer tokens stored in Android DataStore.
  • All network traffic is encrypted over HTTPS.
  • Your password is never stored by the app — it is sent once during login and then discarded.
  • Tokens are cleared from the device when you log out.

Data Deletion

  • Deleting a task in the app removes it locally and propagates the deletion to the server on the next sync.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data from your device.
  • To request deletion of all cloud data tied to your account, email us at the address below.

Third Parties

We do not share your data with any third party for advertising or analytics. The app uses open-source libraries (Jetpack Compose, Room, Retrofit, WorkManager, DataStore) that operate entirely on-device and do not transmit data independently.

Children

Asir Tasks is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy is updated, the effective date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
